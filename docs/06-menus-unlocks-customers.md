# 06 — Menus, Unlocks, Customers & Raid Rules

This is the Zombs BBQ version of every screen, button, unlock and customer, mapped one-to-one to how the original worked (see [01 — Research §3.7–3.12](01-research.md)). Where the original's function was unclear, we chose the most useful one.

## 4. Customer roster

Who walks in = **level + star rating**. Customers inside at once ≈ **1 + your half-stars** (as players measured in the original), capped by seats. Arriving to a full house means they **leave with a blue frowny face**.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Common | **Accountant** | Lv 1 | Free | Balanced; tie stuck in a calculator |
| Common | **Yoga Instructor** | Lv 1 | Free | High Focus (rarely daydreams); permanently stuck in a downward-dog shamble |
| Common | **Teenager** | Lv 1 | Free | Fast, low Patience; earbuds still in |

| Raid-only | Zombie King, Conquistadead, Captain Rotten, Mecha-Muncher, Kitty Corpse | Raid boss drops | — | Parody costume zombies |

**Rare spawn rules:** each customer type has a base weight. Rare and epic weights scale up with stars above the requirement (×1 at the requirement, ×3 at +1★). That's why, as in the original, "rare" customers become common once you're high level with a high rating. When a rare customer arrives, a **sparkle trail** follows them in and a short sting plays. The Zombiepedia entry unlocks on first sight.

## 5. Raid rules (faithful version)

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Raid health | **A zombie's Energy is its health.** Close the joint and rest the crew first. |
| Start | Map → rival (level shown under the building) → choose zombies + pets → they line up on the sidewalk → send one at a time or **Send All Zombies** |
| Control | Tap a target: customer, staff, defense, or **the serving counter (steal a dish)** |
| Eating | Zombies **eat customers and staff** in the rival joint: **cash per bite, kept even if you lose** |
| Defenders | Staff swarm on the first attack. Waiters are weak. The **Head Chef (boss)** comes out when the room is mostly cleared. |
| Win | Head Chef defeated → dishes to the Fridge, cash, recipe unlock chance, rare Brain Sauce (better for fast kills with several zombies at once) |
| Lose | All zombies down → no recipe, but keep the cash from eating |
| Retreat | White flag (top-left) → keep what you've stolen |
| Boosts | Green Grave Marker (+HP), Red Grave Marker (+attack), 5/day each, recharge at local midnight; Healing Headstone (+100 Energy to all, mid-fight); Big Foot-Long summon (max 3); Grill-Bots → Mega Grillatron (all 5, after one zombie has entered the joint) |
| Aftermath | A zombie at 0 Energy is **"down" and reanimates for 1 hour** (the original's later timing), or Brain Sauce to skip. Pets too. Never permanent. |
| Map | 20+ NPC rival joints at launch (9 boss chapters on the campaign road) + friends + PvP neighbors |

## 6. Friends & franchise (faithful version)
- **Add friends** by code, Game Center, or contacts → they appear on your Map. Choose who shows in Friends & Settings.
- **Franchise:** friends can **order a dish from you** (you cook it and they pay a fee) and **share recipes** (study a friend's rare dish).
- **Visit a friend's joint:** **give a gift from your Fridge**, or **sneak one dish off their counter** (once a day; they get a funny notification, "Your buddy stole your Rib Rack!", and a revenge button).
- **Friendly raids:** invited raids with no reanimation penalty.
